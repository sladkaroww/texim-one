Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

TEXIM ONE respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the TEXIM ONE website and services, why it may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

We may receive information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, apply to join TEXIM ONE, participate in our community, or otherwise interact with the website. Depending on the interaction, this may include a name or username, Discord username or identifier, contact information, application information, and other information you choose to provide.

The website may also automatically receive limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and basic usage information when necessary for security, reliability, and site operation.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website.
  • To process applications and communicate with applicants or members.
  • To provide community and event-related services.
  • To protect the website and community against abuse, fraud, and security threats.
  • To understand website usage and improve performance where analytics have been enabled with your consent.

3. Third-Party Services

TEXIM ONE may link to or use third-party services such as Discord, TruckersMP, TrucksBook, Twitch, Steam, Google Fonts, and hosting or analytics providers. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. TEXIM ONE is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services you choose to use.

4. Cookies and Analytics

The website may use essential browser storage or similar technologies required for site functionality. Optional analytics may be used only where enabled and, where applicable, after consent. You can clear or change your browser's stored data at any time.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to operate the community, meet legitimate administrative needs, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about the personal data we hold about you. You may contact TEXIM ONE to make such a request.

7. Children's Privacy

TEXIM ONE does not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will be published on this page with its latest update date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the TEXIM ONE team through our official community channels.
