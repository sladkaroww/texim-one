About TEXIM ONE

Professional virtual trucking logistics and community.

Our Story

TEXIM ONE was founded in 2021 with a clear mission: to provide a professional, realistic, and welcoming virtual trucking experience. What started as a small group of friends hauling cargo across Europe has grown into a thriving community of dedicated drivers.

We focus on realistic convoy operations, strict traffic management, and community-driven events. Our drivers are handpicked for their professionalism and passion for the open road.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To deliver an authentic, organized, and enjoyable virtual trucking experience that brings together drivers from around the world in a professional and friendly environment.

Vision: To become one of the most respected virtual trucking companies, known for our high-quality convoys, custom content, and supportive community.
