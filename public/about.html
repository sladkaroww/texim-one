About TEXIM ONE

Винаги 1 на ум, зад TEXIM! Always number one in mind, behind TEXIM!

Who We Are

TEXIM ONE (TMP VTC #74050) is a Bulgarian-based virtual trucking company founded in 2019. We are an official TruckersMP-recognized VTC supporting both Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator with zero required DLCs.

Our philosophy is simple: accessibility. We don't require monthly km limits, convoy attendance, a minimum age, hours in-game, or a test period. Whether you're a beginner or a veteran, you're welcome to drive on your own terms.

What Makes Us Different

You'll recognize us on the road by our distinct corporate identity, our TEXIM ONE tag, exclusive avatar, and signature truck + trailer composition.

Our recruitment is selective. Every application is reviewed individually, and we value quality and commitment over sheer numbers. Most of our drivers complete World of Trucks deliveries under the speed limiter and without road assistance, keeping the experience realistic and fair.

Recruitment & Community

Applications are handled through our official Google Form, each one reviewed personally. Beyond the road, we run a Twitch team and an official merch line through TEXIM ONE Ltd, making us a well-developed and growing community.

Find us on TruckersMP and join our Discord for everything TEXIM ONE.
